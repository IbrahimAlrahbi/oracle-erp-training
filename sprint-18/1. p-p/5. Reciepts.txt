1. Open:
   Receiving
      -> Receipts

2. Enter:
   Organization: V1 Vision Operations

3. Enter:
   Purchase order no: ‘PO’
   Click <Tab>

4. Add comments.

5. Click:
   <Save>

6. Click:
   Header

7. Note:
   The receipt number will be displayed.

8. In SQL Developer, query:
   Select * from rcv_shipment_headers order by 1 desc;

9. Query the receipt:
   Select * from rcv_shipment_headers where receipt_num = '9119';
